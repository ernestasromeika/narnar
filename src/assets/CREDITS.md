# Audio credits

`water-steps.wav` is adapted from **Splashing Footsteps Shallow Water** by
**ChristopherJngs**, published July 6, 2026 on Freesound:
https://freesound.org/people/ChristopherJngs/sounds/861369/

License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The author's publicly available high-quality MP3 preview was trimmed into four
half-second slots, filtered to reduce rumble and hiss, peak-normalized, and faded
at the edges. The resulting mono WAV is bundled with the game. Playback varies
the sample and its speed slightly; no third-party audio request is made at runtime.
